Diver's Helmet Solver

Paint the coloured arcs on the ball below to match your Meffert's Diver's Helmet, then follow the turns back to four clean rings.

Drag to turn the ball. The brass dot marks the anchor triangle at the bottom-front-left. It never moves.

Step 1

Copy your puzzle

  1. Choose any triangle on your ball as the anchor. Hold it at the bottom-front-left, where the brass dot is.
  2. Pick a colour, then tap the matching spot on the screen ball. Faint grey arcs show every place an arc can sit.
  3. Turn both balls the same way to reach the back. Each colour has 4 arcs.
This is an example scramble. Press Clear and enter your own.
How the puzzle is built

It is a Skewb in a ball. Each turn twists half the ball by a third of a turn around a corner: 3 diamonds and 4 triangles move together. The two plain diamonds (logo and keyring) carry no colour. The other four diamonds each carry two arcs on opposite tips, and every triangle carries one arc. When solved, four rings sit around the middle.

Step 2

Follow the turns

Keep holding the anchor at the bottom-front-left. Each turn twists the half of the ball away from the anchor, around the corner named.
